Brightwater Public Library Notes

The Brightwater Public Library opened a new reading room on the second floor. The reading room has forty seats and extended opening hours on weekdays.

Library membership is free for all residents of Brightwater. Residents can borrow up to ten books at a time for three weeks.

The library hosts a weekly chess club on Thursday evenings and a children's story hour on Saturday mornings.
