Northfield Tram Network Bulletin

The Northfield tram network will add two new stations, Mill Lane and Harbour Gate, at the start of next month. Trams run every ten minutes during rush hour and every twenty minutes in the evening.

A single tram ticket costs two fictional dollars. Monthly passes cost forty fictional dollars. Children under twelve travel free when accompanied by an adult.

The Northfield Transport Authority reported that the tram network carried about nine thousand passengers on an average weekday last year.
