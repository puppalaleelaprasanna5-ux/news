Brightwater Flood Management Summary

The Alder River flows through the fictional town of Brightwater and floods most springs when snowmelt arrives from the northern hills. In the fictional year 1990 the Brightwater Works Department completed a stone levee along the eastern bank of the Alder River. The levee is 4.2 kilometres long.

After the levee was completed, the number of flooded streets in the town fell sharply. A review committee noted that the levee does not protect the western district, which sits on lower ground.

The Works Department recommended a second pumping station for the western district. Funding for the pumping station was approved in a fictional council vote held in the autumn.
